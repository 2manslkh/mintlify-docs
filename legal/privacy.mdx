# Privacy Policy

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to JPEG by JPEG Platform ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our JPEG mobile application (the "App") and related services.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide to Us

*   **Account Information:** When you register for an account, we collect your name, phone number, and any profile information you choose to provide.
*   **User Content:** Information or media you input when creating or interacting with JPEGs, including prompts, edits, comments, likes, images, videos, and audio files. This also includes messages you send through our chat feature.
*   **Communications:** Information you provide when contacting us for support or other inquiries.

### 2.2 Information We Collect Automatically

When you use our App, we may automatically collect:

*   **Device Information:** Device identifiers, operating system, browser type, mobile network information, and IP address.
*   **Usage Information:** How you interact with the App, including JPEGs created, viewed, edited, or shared.
*   **Location Information:** General location information based on IP address.
*   **Analytics Data:** Performance metrics, feature usage patterns, and error reports to improve our services.

### 2.3 AI-Generated Content

*   We collect and process the prompts you provide to generate JPEGs.
*   The resulting code, structures, and designs generated by our AI systems may be stored to enable editing, sharing, and improving our services.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

**We use the information we collect to:**

*   Provide, maintain, and improve our App and services.
*   Create and optimize AI-generated content based on your prompts.
*   Establish and maintain your user profile and account.
*   Enable social features like sharing, commenting, liking JPEGs, and messaging other users.
*   Facilitate private messaging between users and investigate reported violations.
*   Monitor usage patterns and perform analytics to enhance user experience.
*   Respond to your comments, questions, and customer service requests.
*   Send you technical notices, updates, security alerts, and administrative messages.
*   Detect, investigate, and prevent fraudulent transactions and other illegal activities.
*   Comply with legal obligations.

## 4. Information Sharing and Disclosure

**We may share your information with:**

*   **Service Providers:** Third parties that help us operate our business and provide our services, including cloud hosting providers, analytics services, and AI model providers.
*   **Other Users:** When you publish a JPEG, your username and any information associated with that JPEG becomes visible to other users.
*   **Affiliates:** Our subsidiaries and affiliates to support our business operations.
*   **As Required by Law:** To comply with legal obligations, protect rights, safety, and property, and respond to lawful requests from public authorities.
*   **Business Transfers:** In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

## 5. User Content and JPEGs

### User-Generated JPEGs

*   When you create and publish a JPEG, it becomes accessible to other users of the App.
*   You retain ownership rights to the content you create, but grant us a license as described in our Terms of Service.

### Content Sharing & Remixing

*   When you publish a JPEG, other users may be able to view, share, and potentially remix your creations.
*   When you remix another user's JPEG, appropriate attribution will be provided to the original creator.

### Content Moderation

*   We reserve the right to remove content that violates our Terms of Service.
*   We may use automated systems and human reviewers to monitor user-generated content.

## 6. Messaging and Chat Privacy

Our App includes messaging features that allow you to communicate directly with other users. Messages sent through our chat feature are private between you and the recipient(s). We do not proactively monitor the content of private messages.

However, if another user reports a chat message for violating our Terms of Service or Community Guidelines, our moderation team may access and review the reported message and surrounding context to investigate the report and enforce our policies. We retain chat messages in accordance with our data retention policies as described in Section 14 of this Privacy Policy.

## 7. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

For your protection, we strongly advise against sharing sensitive personal information through our App, including but not limited to health information, financial details, government identification numbers, or other confidential data. Remember that content you create may be visible to other users if shared, and may be accessible to JPEG Platform employees even if not shared publicly. While we take steps to protect your information, you should exercise caution when deciding what to include in your JPEGs.

## 8. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including:

*   Accessing, correcting, or deleting your personal information.
*   Restricting or objecting to certain processing activities.
*   Data portability.
*   Withdrawing consent where processing is based on consent.

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the information in the "Contact Us" section.

### Account Information

You can update your account information directly through the App's profile settings.

### Push Notifications

You can opt out of receiving push notifications through your device settings.

### Cookies and Similar Technologies

You can set your browser to refuse cookies or alert you when cookies are being sent.

## 9. Children's Privacy

Our App is not directed to children under 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we learn we have collected personal information from a child without parental consent, we will delete that information. If you believe we have collected information from a child, please contact us.

## 10. Third-Party AI Model Usage

Our service utilizes third-party AI models to generate content based on your prompts and to power features in the App. Information provided to these models is subject to our agreements with these providers and is primarily used for providing our services, including but not limited to generating JPEGs and facilitating conversations. This information may also be used for other purposes as outlined in this Privacy Policy.

## 11. International Data Transfers

We are based in the United States and process information on servers located in the United States. If you are accessing our App from outside the United States, your information may be transferred to, stored, and processed in the United States or other countries where our service providers maintain facilities.

## 12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page, updating the "Last Updated" date, and, where appropriate, notifying you through the App or via email.

## 13. California Privacy Rights

If you are a California resident, you have specific rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), including:

*   Right to know what personal information we collect and how we use it.
*   Right to delete your personal information.
*   Right to opt-out of the sale or sharing of your personal information.
*   Right to non-discrimination for exercising your privacy rights.

## 14. App Store Compliance

Our App complies with applicable app marketplace requirements, including relevant privacy standards for mobile applications. We adhere to platform-specific guidelines and requirements for user privacy and data handling.

## 15. Content Deletion and Data Retention

When you delete content from the App (including JPEGs, comments, or other user-generated content), it is immediately removed from public view and becomes inaccessible to other users. However, deleted content may remain in our backup systems and servers to comply with legal obligations, prevent fraud and abuse, maintain platform security, and support abuse investigations.

We retain personal information and deleted content for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law. After our retention period, content is permanently deleted from our systems.

## 16. International Users

If you are accessing our App from countries outside the United States, your information may be governed by both the laws of your country and those of the United States. This Privacy Policy is intended to be consistent with applicable privacy laws. If you believe your local privacy laws provide you with additional rights, please contact us using the information in the "Contact Us" section.
