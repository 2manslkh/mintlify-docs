## From App to Network

JPEG is designed to evolve from a consumer app into an **open network** where JPEG is the standard primitive for photo assets. Images become programmable objects rather than disposable posts.

### Current State: Application

Today, JPEG functions as a social prediction game:

- Daily themed photo contests
- Collect mechanics for prediction markets
- Freestyle JPEG creation and trading
- Creator royalties and rewards

### Future State: Network

JPEG will become a **composable network** where:

- JPEGs are standard photo assets
- Assets can be owned, traded, and built upon
- Developers create new markets and experiences
- Entrepreneurs build complementary services
- Traders and collectors participate across applications

## True Social

**True Social** means attention brought by users should be fairly rewarded. Capital flows replace opaque algorithms, revealing cultural value through market participation rather than extraction.

### Key Principles

1. **Transparency:** Market mechanisms are visible and auditable
2. **Fair Rewards:** Creators receive value proportional to attention
3. **Capital-Backed:** Attention is backed by real economic value
4. **User Ownership:** Value accrues to users, not platforms

### Market as Algorithm

In JPEG, the market is the sorting mechanism. The market is the algorithm. This means:

- No hidden ranking factors
- No algorithmic manipulation
- Collective decisions determine what rises
- Capital flows reveal true cultural resonance

## Composable Photo Assets

JPEGs can be **traded, coded, and built upon**. Composability enables third-party innovation, allowing developers and entrepreneurs to create new markets and experiences on top of shared cultural assets.

### What Makes JPEGs Composable?

**Ownership:**

- JPEGs are on-chain assets
- Ownership is verifiable and transferable
- Assets persist across applications

**Standards:**

- JPEG becomes a standard primitive
- Interoperable across the network
- Programmable and extensible

**Permissionless:**

- No approval needed to integrate
- Open APIs and protocols
- Developer-friendly infrastructure

### Use Cases

**Trading Platforms:**

- Secondary markets for JPEGs
- Cross-application trading
- Market aggregation services

**Developer Tools:**

- SDKs for JPEG integration
- APIs for asset queries
- Building blocks for new apps

**New Markets:**

- Curated collections
- Themed marketplaces
- Specialized trading venues

**Enterprise Integration:**

- Brand asset management
- UGC licensing platforms
- Data annotation services

### Key Distinction

> Platforms compete with complements, networks empower them.

This means JPEG is designed to enable all participants to benefit from network growth without fear of being shut down or cannibalized.
