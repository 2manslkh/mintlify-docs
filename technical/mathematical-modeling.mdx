## Shares-for-Fees Model

The JPEG economy operates on a **Shares-for-Fees** model, ensuring 100% of the PX (Pixel) currency deposited by users is immediately allocated to the global prize pool. Platform and creator fees are paid in the form of shares, introducing controlled dilution to winning potential rather than reducing capital available for rewards. This mechanism guarantees capital efficiency and full transparency.

### Key Variables

| Variable | Definition                                                    | Unit     |
| :------- | :------------------------------------------------------------ | :------- |
| `B`      | Individual Bet Amount (ranging from 1 to Max Bet Limit)       | PX       |
| `∑B`     | Sum of all Bets (PX) placed across all photos                 | PX       |
| `F`      | **Foundation Funding:** Fixed PX injected into the prize pool | PX       |
| `t_bet`  | Time the bet is placed, normalized (0 to 1)                   | Unitless |
| `t_post` | Time the photo was posted, normalized `t ∈ [0, 1)`            | Unitless |
| `D_max`  | Maximum initial discount (e.g., `0.99 → 99%`)                 | Unitless |
| `R_C`    | **Creator Share Rate:** (e.g., `0.20 → 20%`)                  | Unitless |
| `R_F`    | **Foundation Share Rate:** (e.g., `0.10 → 10%`)               | Unitless |

## Time Decay Mechanism

The time decay formula governs collect value for themed photos, creating strategic incentives for early participation.

### Quadratic Time Discount (D_t)

The system uses a **quadratic time discount** model. The initial discount (`D_max`) decays quadratically as the game window closes (`t_bet → 1`). The decay is calculated relative to the photo's lifespan (from `t_post` to `t=1`).

**Quadratic Time Discount Formula:**

```
D_t = D_max · (1 - (t_bet - t_post) / (1.0 - t_post))²
```

**Example with D_max = 0.99:**

**Scenario A (Post at 00:00):**

- A bet placed at 00:00 receives the full 99% discount
- A bet placed at 12:00 (halfway through the day) receives a ~25% discount (`0.99 · 0.5²`)

**Scenario B (Post at 12:00):**

- A bet placed immediately at 12:00 receives the full 99% discount
- A bet placed at 18:00 (halfway through the remaining window) receives a ~25% discount

<Info>
  Earlier posts (e.g., 00:00) offer a larger window of opportunity and a greater
  cumulative discount "mass" than later posts. This incentivizes creators to
  post early to maximize the time window for high-value collects.
</Info>

<Warning>
  `t_post` must be strictly less than 1.0. A post submitted at the exact end of
  the day (`t=1`) allows for no betting window and is therefore undefined in
  this model.
</Warning>

## Share Issuance

For every bet (`B`), three distinct categories of shares are issued. `D_t` creates the primary incentive for early participation.

### Share Allocation Formulas

| Share Type                  | Formula             | Description                                                                 |
| :-------------------------- | :------------------ | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **User Shares (S)**         | `S = B · (1 + D_t)` | Shares received by the collector. Represents the user's equity in the pool. |
| **Creator Shares (S_C)**    | `S_C = B · R_C`     | Shares allocated to the photo's creator (fee taken as equity dilution).     |
| **Foundation Shares (S_F)** | `S_F = B · R_F`     | Shares allocated to the JPEG Foundation (fee taken as equity dilution).     |

The **Total Shares Issued per transaction** (`S_Total`) for this photo is:

```
S_Total = B · (1 + D_t + R_C + R_F)
```

## Prize Pool Distribution

### Prize Pool Contribution (P)

The Prize Pool (`P`) is the total capital available to be distributed to the shareholders of the winning photo. Every PX bet by a user directly feeds this pool.

```
P = (∑ All User Bets) + F
```

Where `F` is optional Foundation Funding that can be injected into the prize pool.

### Payout Calculation

The **Value per Share (V)** is the final price of one share. It is determined by the total Prize Pool (`P`) divided by the total shares issued _only_ by the winning photo (`∑ S_Total, Win`).

```
V = P / (∑ Winning Photo S_Total)
```

The final payout to any stakeholder is calculated by multiplying their accumulated shares by the final share value (`V`).

```
Stakeholder Payout = Stakeholder Shares · V
```

### Economic Implications

The system creates an internal, dynamic share price (`V`) which is inherently less than the initial user capital contribution (since `R_C` and `R_F` dilute the pool) unless the prize pool is significantly boosted by losing bets or Foundation Funding (`F`).

This dilution incentivizes users to secure the maximum possible `D_t` bonus. It ensures that the earliest collectors can receive a higher positive return on investment, aligning with the **"higher risk, higher reward"** game design and preventing last-minute sniping.

## Mathematical Properties

### Time Decay Characteristics

**Quadratic Decay:**

- Accelerating rate of decay as time progresses
- Strong early incentive (high discount at t=0)
- Rewards early risk-taking more aggressively than linear models
- Creates larger cumulative discount "mass" for early posts

### Share Distribution

Shares are distributed proportionally based on accumulated shares:

```
User Payout = User Shares · V
```

Where `V` is the value per share calculated from the winning photo's total shares.

This ensures fair distribution based on:

- Collection timing (via `D_t` discount)
- Bet amount (`B`)
- Number of collects per user
- Winning photo selection

## Optimization Considerations

### Discount Parameter Selection

The maximum discount (`D_max`) and share rates (`R_C`, `R_F`) balance:

- Early collect incentives (via `D_max`)
- Creator incentives (via `R_C`, typically 20%)
- Platform sustainability (via `R_F`, typically 10%)
- Capital efficiency (100% of capital to prize pool)
- User retention

**Current Parameters:**

- `D_max = 0.99` (99% maximum discount)
- `R_C = 0.20` (20% creator share rate)
- `R_F = 0.10` (10% foundation share rate)

### Prize Pool Efficiency

The Shares-for-Fees model optimizes:

- **Capital Efficiency:** 100% of user capital goes to prize pool
- **Transparency:** All fees visible through share dilution
- **Fairness:** Early collectors rewarded through time discount
- **Sustainability:** Platform and creator fees via controlled dilution

<Note>
  Platform may adjust share rates (`R_C`, `R_F`) and discount parameters
  (`D_max`) based on metrics and user feedback, but the core Shares-for-Fees
  mechanism ensures capital efficiency.
</Note>
